# SvaraCall AI — Enterprise Programmatic SEO Blueprint

**Horizon:** 24–36 months  
**Goal:** Become the most authoritative AI voice agent resource across Google, AI search engines, and voice search.  
**Base URL today:** `https://svaracall.sarwagyna.com`  
**Current indexable surface:** ~34 URLs (11 industries, 10 languages, hubs)  
**Keyword idea matrix:** ~145,500+ (deduped unique intents ~60–90k)  
**Indexable page target (quality-gated):** Phase 1 → 500 · Phase 5 → 200,000+

> **Non-negotiable:** Every URL must pass the Quality Gate (unique data, workflow, FAQs, schema, internal links, EEAT). Combinatorial scale without uniqueness is a spam risk under Google’s Helpful Content System and Search Essentials.

---

## Executive summary

SvaraCall already has a strong **programmatic seed**: `/industries/[slug]`, `/languages/[slug]`, plus catalogs for solutions and ~90 integrations that lack per-item routes. The winning strategy is:

1. **Own the category pillars** (AI Voice Agent, AI Calling Software, AI Receptionist, Voice AI Platform).
2. **Productize content** as reusable modules (pain → workflow → ROI → compliance → FAQ → schema).
3. **Expand along high-intent axes first** (use cases, features, comparisons, integrations).
4. **Then explode niches × geos × languages** only where demand + uniqueness justify indexing.
5. **Win AI search** with entity consistency, citation magnets, and answer-first blocks.

## STEP 4 — Industry page specification

### 4.1 Required sections (every industry)

1. **Hero** — industry-specific promise + CTA (Book pilot / Demo call)
2. **Pain points** — 5–8 quantified pains (missed calls, no-shows, recovery lag)
3. **Manual workflow** — swimlane of today’s human process
4. **AI workflow** — SvaraCall flow with decision points + human handoff
5. **ROI model** — calculator inputs (agents, calls/day, conversion, AHT, cost/call)
6. **KPIs** — containment, connect rate, booking rate, recovery %, CSAT, compliance rate
7. **Compliance** — industry + geo (HIPAA/DPDP/IRDAI/RBI/TRAI/TCPA as relevant)
8. **Integrations** — top 6–10 for that vertical
9. **Case study** — named or anonymized with metrics (or “pilot design” until real)

11. **Related** — use cases, features, languages, compare, templates
12. **CTA band** — pilot + WhatsApp

### 4.3 Sample outline — Banking / NBFC

- Manual: collector dialer → script → disposition → CRM  
- AI: prioritized dial · vernacular negotiation · promise-to-pay · payment link · escalation

- Compliance: RBI guidelines, recording consent, DND, abusive-language filters

- FAQs: Is AI collection legal in India? Can it speak Hindi/Tamil? PCI for card data?

Repeat this module for every industry — **swap data, keep structure**.

- [ ] Unique H1 + unique primary intent (not a synonym of a sibling)
- [ ] ≥1 original data point, workflow, or script branch
- [ ] Industry/niche-specific examples
- [ ] FAQ (3+) visible + FAQ schema
- [ ] Breadcrumb schema
- [ ] ≥6 purposeful internal links
- [ ] CTA to Book Pilot + secondary contact
- [ ] Author/updated date on informational content
- [ ] Compliance note when outbound/collections/health
