# SvaraCall — Subprocessor List

**Sarwagyna Private Limited**
**Last Updated: 12 September 2026**
**Version 1.0**

This page lists the third parties we engage to help deliver the SvaraCall platform. It is incorporated into our Privacy Policy and Data Processing Addendum.

We give **thirty (30) days' notice** before adding or replacing any Subprocessor that processes call content or contact data. To receive notifications, email [privacy@svaracall.com](mailto:privacy@svaracall.com).

---

## Current Subprocessors

| # | Subprocessor | Function | Data processed | Processing location |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vobiz | Telephony carriage, number provisioning, SIP trunking | Phone numbers, call metadata, call audio in transit | India |
| 2 | Sarvam AI | Speech-to-text and text-to-speech | Call audio, transcripts | India |
| 3 | Groq, Inc. | Large language model inference | Transcript text, prompts, knowledge base excerpts, agent responses | United States |
| 4 | Amazon Web Services, Inc. | Cloud hosting and storage of platform data, including call recordings | Account, campaign, contact, recording, and transcript data at rest | India (Mumbai) |
| 5 | Vercel Inc. | Frontend and edge hosting | Dashboard session data, request logs | Global edge |

## Data Residency Summary

| Data category | Stored in India? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Account and billing data | Yes | Stored with our India-region cloud infrastructure |
| Contact lists uploaded by customers | Yes | Stored with our India-region cloud infrastructure |
| Call recordings at rest | Yes | Stored in AWS Mumbai |
| Transcripts at rest | Yes | Stored in AWS Mumbai |
| Text sent for LLM inference | No | Processed by Groq in the United States |
| Call audio in transit | Yes | Telephony and speech processing in India |

**Current position:** SvaraCall is not a fully in-country stack. Language model inference and parts of the hosting layer process data outside India. Customers requiring full in-country processing should contact us before contracting — we cannot offer it on the standard platform today.

---

## Model Training

We do not use identifiable customer call audio, transcripts, or contact data to train general-purpose AI models. Where a Subprocessor's default terms permit training on submitted data, we configure the no-training or zero-retention option where the provider offers one. For a current statement on a named Subprocessor, email [privacy@svaracall.com](mailto:privacy@svaracall.com).
